# AOV sound files

The six MP3 files here are original replacements for the earlier soundboard clips. AOV plays them locally at sign-on, sign-off, new mail, and chat events.

`dialup.mp3`, `message.mp3`, and `sent.mp3` are synthesized from code in `scripts/generate-interface-tones.py`. They contain no sampled recording and are provided under GPLv3 with that generator.

`welcome.mp3`, `mail.mp3`, and `goodbye.mp3` are original AOV lines generated with ElevenLabs using the Anna voice. Attribution: [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/). The lines are “Welcome to America On Vibe,” “You have new mail,” and “See you next time on America On Vibe.” These voice assets are for noncommercial use with attribution and are **not** licensed under GPLv3. Replace them with your own licensed voice clips before commercial redistribution.
